```gherkin
Feature: Restaurant Quick Rating Stars on Card
  As a logged-in user browsing restaurants
  I want to quickly rate a restaurant from its card on the homepage
  So that I can share my rating without navigating to the detail page

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Show quick rating overlay on hover when logged in
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    Then I should see the quick rating overlay with data-testid "quick-rating-overlay"
    And I should see 5 interactive star buttons with data-testid "quick-rating-star-1" through "quick-rating-star-5"

  @unit-test @priority-high @edge-case
  Scenario: Hide quick rating overlay when logged out
    Given I am not logged in
    And I am on the homepage
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    Then I should NOT see the quick rating overlay with data-testid "quick-rating-overlay"
    And the restaurant card should display normally without rating stars

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Open confirmation dialog when clicking a star
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    And I hover over the first restaurant card
    When I click on the 4-star rating button with data-testid "quick-rating-star-4"
    Then I should see the rating confirmation dialog with data-testid "quick-rating-dialog"
    And the dialog should display the restaurant name from the hovered card
    And the dialog should show exactly 4 filled star icons and 1 empty star icon
    And I should see a confirm button with data-testid "quick-rating-confirm" and text "Submit Rating"
    And I should see a cancel button with data-testid "quick-rating-cancel" and text "Cancel"

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated @integration
  Scenario: Submit rating and show success toast when confirmed
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    And the first restaurant card displays a restaurant with id "rest-001"
    And I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click on the 5-star rating button
    And the rating confirmation dialog is visible
    When I click the confirm button with data-testid "quick-rating-confirm"
    Then the confirm button should show loading spinner for up to 3 seconds
    And I should see a success toast with text "Rating submitted!"
    And the rating confirmation dialog should close
    And the restaurant card with id "rest-001" should display the updated average rating

  @unit-test @priority-medium @authenticated @edge-case
  Scenario: Show error toast when rating submission fails due to network error
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    And the API endpoint "/api/reviews" is configured to return a 500 error
    And I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click on the 4-star rating button
    And the rating confirmation dialog is visible
    When I click the confirm button with data-testid "quick-rating-confirm"
    Then I should see an error toast with text "Failed to submit rating. Please try again."
    And the rating confirmation dialog should remain open
    And the confirm button should be enabled for retry

  @unit-test @priority-medium @authenticated @integration
  Scenario: Rating persists after page navigation and return
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    And the first restaurant card displays a restaurant named "Bella Italia"
    And I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click on the 4-star rating button
    And I confirm the rating submission
    And I see the success toast "Rating submitted!"
    When I navigate to the restaurant detail page for "Bella Italia"
    And I return to the homepage by clicking the logo
    Then the restaurant card for "Bella Italia" should display my 4-star rating indicator
    And hovering over the card should show my existing rating highlighted in the quick rating overlay

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium @authenticated
  Scenario: Visual validation of quick rating overlay on card hover
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    Then the page should visually show the quick rating overlay with 5 star icons
    And the overlay should be positioned at the bottom of the card image
    And all 5 stars should be unfilled with gray color initially
    And hovering over the 3rd star should fill stars 1 through 3 with gold color

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium @authenticated
  Scenario: Visual validation of rating confirmation dialog
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage
    And I hover over the first restaurant card
    When I click on the 4-star rating button
    Then the page should visually show the confirmation dialog centered on screen
    And the dialog should display exactly 4 filled gold stars and 1 empty gray star
    And the dialog should show the restaurant name in bold text above the stars
    And the confirm button should be styled with primary blue background color
    And the cancel button should be styled with secondary gray background color
    And the page background should have a semi-transparent dark overlay
```
