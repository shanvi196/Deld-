```gherkin
Feature: Price Range Visual Indicator
  As a user browsing restaurants
  I want to see a visual price scale instead of text dollar signs
  So that I can quickly understand price levels at a glance

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: PriceScale displays correct number of filled indicators for level 2
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    When I view a restaurant card with price level 2 ($$)
    Then the element with data-testid="price-scale" should be visible
    And there should be exactly 2 elements with data-testid="price-scale-filled"
    And there should be exactly 2 elements with data-testid="price-scale-empty"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: PriceScale displays correct number of filled indicators for level 4
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    When I view a restaurant card with price level 4 ($$$$)
    Then the element with data-testid="price-scale" should be visible
    And there should be exactly 4 elements with data-testid="price-scale-filled"
    And there should be exactly 0 elements with data-testid="price-scale-empty"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Tooltip shows correct price range on hover for level 1
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    When I hover over a PriceScale element with data-testid="price-scale" for a level 1 restaurant
    Then the tooltip with data-testid="price-scale-tooltip" should become visible
    And the tooltip should contain the text "Under $15"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Tooltip shows correct price range on hover for level 3
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    When I hover over a PriceScale element with data-testid="price-scale" for a level 3 restaurant
    Then the tooltip with data-testid="price-scale-tooltip" should become visible
    And the tooltip should contain the text "$30-$60"

  @unit-test @edge-case
  Scenario: Tooltip hides when mouse leaves PriceScale
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    And I have hovered over a PriceScale to show the tooltip
    When I move the mouse away from the PriceScale element
    Then the tooltip with data-testid="price-scale-tooltip" should not be visible

  @unit-test @edge-case
  Scenario: PriceScale renders consistently across multiple restaurant cards with different price levels
    Given I am on the homepage with restaurant listings
    When I view the restaurant listing grid containing cards with price levels 1, 2, 3, and 4
    Then each restaurant card should display exactly 4 total price indicators (filled + empty combined)
    And the first restaurant card with level 1 should have exactly 1 filled and 3 empty indicators
    And the restaurant card with level 3 should have exactly 3 filled and 1 empty indicator

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of PriceScale component on restaurant cards
    Given I am on the homepage at "http://localhost:3000"
    When the page has fully loaded with at least 3 restaurant cards visible
    Then each restaurant card should display a visual price scale with exactly 4 dollar sign indicators
    And filled indicators should use a solid/highlighted style distinct from empty indicators
    And the price scale should be horizontally aligned within the restaurant card metadata section

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of PriceScale tooltip positioning and styling
    Given I am on the homepage at "http://localhost:3000"
    And at least 1 restaurant card is visible
    When I hover over the price scale indicator on the first restaurant card
    Then the tooltip with data-testid="price-scale-tooltip" should appear within 300ms
    And the tooltip should be positioned directly above or below the price scale element
    And the tooltip text should be readable with sufficient contrast against its background
```
