```gherkin
Feature: Dietary Compatibility Score
  As a user with dietary restrictions
  I want to see how well restaurants match my dietary needs
  So that I can quickly identify suitable dining options

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: No dietary score badge displayed when user is not logged in
    Given I am on the homepage without being logged in
    When the restaurant cards load
    Then no elements with data-testid="dietary-score-badge" should be visible
    And restaurant cards should display normally without score indicators

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Dietary score badge displayed when logged in user has dietary restrictions
    Given I am logged in as a user with dietary restrictions ["vegetarian", "gluten-free"]
    And I am on the homepage
    When the restaurant cards load
    Then at least one element with data-testid="dietary-score-badge" should be visible
    And the badges should appear on restaurant cards

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Dietary score badge displays percentage value
    Given I am logged in as a user with dietary restrictions ["vegetarian"]
    And I am on the homepage
    When the restaurant cards load
    Then the element with data-testid="dietary-score-value" should be visible
    And the score value should match the pattern "\d+%"
    And the percentage should be between 0 and 100

  @unit-test @edge-case @authenticated
  Scenario: No badge displayed when user has empty dietary restrictions
    Given I am logged in as a user with empty dietary restrictions []
    And I am on the homepage
    When the restaurant cards load
    Then no elements with data-testid="dietary-score-badge" should be visible
    And restaurant cards should display without score indicators

  @unit-test @integration @authenticated
  Scenario: Dietary score badge updates when user logs out from homepage
    Given I am logged in as a user with dietary restrictions ["vegetarian", "gluten-free"]
    And I am on the homepage
    And at least one element with data-testid="dietary-score-badge" is visible
    When I click the logout button in the navigation
    And I wait for the page to update
    Then no elements with data-testid="dietary-score-badge" should be visible
    And restaurant cards should remain visible without score indicators

  @unit-test @integration @authenticated
  Scenario: Dietary score recalculates when navigating back to homepage after profile update
    Given I am logged in as a user with dietary restrictions ["vegetarian"]
    And I am on the homepage
    And a restaurant exists with dietaryOptions ["vegetarian", "vegan"]
    And the dietary score badge for that restaurant displays "100%"
    When I navigate to the profile settings page
    And I update my dietary restrictions to ["vegetarian", "gluten-free"]
    And I save the profile changes
    And I navigate back to the homepage
    Then the dietary score badge for that restaurant should display "50%"
    And the badge should have yellow color styling indicating medium compatibility

  @screenshot-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Visual validation of dietary score badges on restaurant cards
    Given I am logged in as a user with dietary restrictions ["vegetarian", "gluten-free"]
    And I am on the homepage
    When the page fully loads
    Then the page should show restaurant cards with circular dietary compatibility score badges
    And each badge should display a percentage value inside
    And badges should be visually distinct and properly positioned on cards

  @screenshot-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Visual validation that badges do not appear for logged out users
    Given I am not logged in
    And I am on the homepage
    When the page fully loads
    Then restaurant cards should be visible without any dietary score badges
    And cards should show normal content including image, name, rating, and cuisine
```
