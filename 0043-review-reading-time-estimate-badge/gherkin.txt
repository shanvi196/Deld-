```gherkin
Feature: Review Reading Time Estimate Badge
  As a user browsing restaurant reviews
  I want to see an estimated reading time for each review
  So that I can gauge how long a review will take to read before diving in

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Reading time badge displays on review cards
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And the page has fully loaded with reviews visible
    When I look at the first review card
    Then I should see an element with data-testid="reading-time-badge"
    And the reading time badge should contain text matching pattern "\d+ min read"
    And the badge should appear in the same row as the review date

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Reading time calculates correctly for medium-length reviews
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And the page has fully loaded with reviews visible
    When I examine a review with approximately 400 words of text
    Then the reading time badge should display "2 min read"
    And the badge should use the Tag component styling (neutral-100 background, neutral-500 text)

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Short reviews show minimum 1 minute reading time
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And there is a review with less than 200 words
    When I view that review's reading time badge
    Then the badge with data-testid="reading-time-badge" should display "1 min read"
    And not display "0 min read" or fractional minutes

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Reading time badge appears on all review cards in list
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And the reviews list section is visible
    When I count elements with data-testid="reading-time-badge"
    Then each visible review card should have exactly one reading time badge
    And the count of badges should equal the count of review cards

  @unit-test @edge-case
  Scenario: Reading time badge persists after review list refresh
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And I can see 3 review cards with reading time badges displayed
    When I trigger a page refresh by pressing F5
    And the page reloads and reviews section becomes visible again
    Then all 3 review cards should still display their reading time badges
    And the badge values should match the original calculated times

  @unit-test @edge-case
  Scenario: Reading time badge renders correctly when navigating between restaurant pages
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And the first review shows a reading time badge with data-testid="reading-time-badge"
    When I navigate to a different restaurant page "/restaurants/rest_0002"
    And the new page loads with its reviews section visible
    Then the reading time badges should display values calculated from the new restaurant's reviews
    And no stale badge data from "/restaurants/rest_0001" should appear

  @screenshot-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Visual validation of reading time badge on restaurant reviews
    Given I navigate directly to "http://localhost:3000/restaurants/rest_0001"
    And I wait for the page to fully load with reviews visible
    When I scroll to the reviews section
    Then the page should visually show reading time badges next to review dates
    And badges should appear as small neutral-colored tags
    And the layout should show stars, date, and reading time badge in a horizontal row

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of reading time badge styling
    Given I navigate directly to "http://localhost:3000/restaurants/rest_0002"
    And I wait for the reviews section to load
    When I examine the review card layout
    Then the reading time badge should be visually consistent with the Tag component design
    And the badge should not disrupt the existing review card layout
    And text should be readable with appropriate font size and contrast
```
