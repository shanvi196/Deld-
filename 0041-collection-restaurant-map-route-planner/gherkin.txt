Feature: Collection Restaurant Route Planner
  As a user with a restaurant collection
  I want to plan an optimized visiting route for my saved restaurants
  So that I can efficiently visit multiple restaurants on a food tour

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"
    And I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Plan Route button click triggers route calculation and displays polyline

    And the polyline should have exactly 3 coordinate points connecting the markers
    And the "Plan Route" button text should change to "Clear Route"
