Feature: Photo Lightbox with Navigation
  As a user browsing restaurant reviews
  I want to click on review photos to view them in a fullscreen lightbox
  So that I can see photos at larger size with easy navigation

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Open lightbox by clicking review photo
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I scroll to the reviews section
    And there is at least one review with photos visible
    When I click on the first photo in a review card
    Then the lightbox modal with data-testid="photo-lightbox-modal" should be visible
    And the lightbox should display an image with data-testid="lightbox-photo"
    And a close button with data-testid="lightbox-close-button" should be visible

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Close lightbox with close button
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I have opened the lightbox by clicking a review photo
    And the lightbox modal with data-testid="photo-lightbox-modal" is visible
    When I click the close button with data-testid="lightbox-close-button"
    Then the lightbox modal should not be visible
    And the restaurant page content should be visible behind

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Close lightbox with Escape key
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I have opened the lightbox by clicking a review photo
    And the lightbox modal with data-testid="photo-lightbox-modal" is visible
    When I press the "Escape" key
    Then the lightbox modal should not be visible
    And the restaurant page content should be visible

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Navigate to next photo with right arrow key
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I find a review with at least 3 photos
    And I have opened the lightbox on the first photo of that review
    And the photo counter with data-testid="lightbox-photo-counter" shows "1 of 3"
    When I press the "ArrowRight" key
    Then the photo counter should show "2 of 3"
    And a different photo should be displayed in the lightbox

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Navigate to previous photo with left arrow key
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I find a review with at least 3 photos
    And I have opened the lightbox and navigated to the second photo
    And the photo counter with data-testid="lightbox-photo-counter" shows "2 of 3"
    When I press the "ArrowLeft" key
    Then the photo counter should show "1 of 3"
    And the first photo should be displayed in the lightbox

  @unit-test @edge-case
  Scenario: Lightbox state resets when opening different review photos
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I find a review with at least 3 photos
    And I have opened the lightbox and navigated to "3 of 3"
    And I close the lightbox by pressing the "Escape" key
    When I click on the first photo of a different review card
    Then the lightbox modal with data-testid="photo-lightbox-modal" should be visible
    And the photo counter with data-testid="lightbox-photo-counter" should show "1 of" followed by that review's photo count
    And the displayed photo should be the first photo from the newly clicked review

  @screenshot-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Visual validation of lightbox modal appearance
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I scroll to the reviews section with photos
    When I click on a review photo to open the lightbox
    Then the page should visually show a centered modal overlay with semi-transparent backdrop
    And the photo should be displayed prominently in the center
    And a close button should be visible in the corner
    And a photo counter should be visible showing position

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of lightbox with photo counter
    Given I am on restaurant details page "/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And I find a review with at least 2 photos
    When I open the lightbox on that review's photos
    Then the page should visually show the lightbox with photo counter in "X of Y" format
    And the counter should be clearly visible against the modal background
