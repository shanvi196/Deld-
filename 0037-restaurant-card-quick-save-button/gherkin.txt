Feature: Restaurant Card Quick Save Button
  As a user browsing restaurants on the homepage
  I want to save restaurants to my collections without leaving the list view
  So that I can quickly bookmark interesting restaurants while browsing

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Quick save button appears on restaurant card hover
    Given I am on the homepage with at least 3 restaurant cards visible
    When I hover over a restaurant card with data-testid "restaurant-card-link-rest_0001"
    Then a quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0001" should be visible
    And the button should be positioned in the top-right corner of the card
    And the button should have an outline bookmark icon
    And the other restaurant cards should NOT display their quick save buttons

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Quick save button opens SaveToCollectionDialog when clicked
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage with at least 1 restaurant card visible
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click the quick save button with data-testid starting with "quick-save-button-"
    Then the SaveToCollectionDialog with data-testid "save-to-collection-dialog" should be visible
    And the dialog should display at least 1 existing collection
    And a text input with data-testid "collection-name-input" should be visible for creating new collections

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Quick save button shows login dialog for unauthenticated users
    Given I am not logged in
    And I am on the homepage with at least 1 restaurant card visible
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click the quick save button with data-testid starting with "quick-save-button-"
    Then the login dialog with data-testid "login-dialog" should appear within 500ms
    And the current URL should be exactly "http://localhost:3000"
    And the restaurant cards should still be visible behind the dialog overlay

  @unit-test @priority-medium @authenticated
  Scenario: Bookmark icon shows filled state for saved restaurants
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I have restaurant "rest_0001" saved in the collection "Favorites"
    And I am on the homepage with restaurant card "rest_0001" visible
    When I hover over the restaurant card with data-testid "restaurant-card-link-rest_0001"
    Then the quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0001" should display a filled bookmark icon
    And the button should have aria-label "Remove from collection" or "Saved to collection"

  @unit-test @priority-medium @authenticated
  Scenario: Quick save button state synchronizes after saving to multiple collections
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I have exactly 2 collections named "Favorites" and "Date Night"
    And restaurant "rest_0002" is NOT saved in any collection
    And I am on the homepage with restaurant card "rest_0002" visible
    And the quick save button for "rest_0002" displays an outline bookmark icon
    When I click the quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0002"
    And the SaveToCollectionDialog appears with both collections unchecked
    And I check the collection "Favorites"
    And I check the collection "Date Night"
    And I close the dialog by clicking outside the dialog overlay
    Then the quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0002" should display a filled bookmark icon
    And reopening the SaveToCollectionDialog for "rest_0002" should show both "Favorites" and "Date Night" as checked

  @unit-test @priority-medium @authenticated @edge-case
  Scenario: Quick save button state updates when removing restaurant from all collections
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I have restaurant "rest_0001" saved in exactly 1 collection named "Favorites"
    And I am on the homepage with restaurant card "rest_0001" visible
    And the quick save button for "rest_0001" displays a filled bookmark icon
    When I click the quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0001"
    And the SaveToCollectionDialog appears showing "Favorites" as checked
    And I uncheck the collection "Favorites" to remove the restaurant
    And I close the dialog by clicking the close button
    Then the quick save button with data-testid "quick-save-button-rest_0001" should now display an outline bookmark icon
    And the restaurant "rest_0001" should no longer be in any collection

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of quick save button on hover
    Given I am on the homepage with at least 3 restaurant cards loaded
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    Then the page should visually show a bookmark icon button in the top-right corner of the first card only
    And the second and third restaurant cards should NOT show their quick save buttons
    And the quick save button should have visible contrast against the card background image

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium @authenticated
  Scenario: Visual validation of SaveToCollectionDialog opened from card
    Given I am logged in as "sarah.chen@email.com" with password "password123"
    And I am on the homepage with at least 1 restaurant card visible
    When I hover over the first restaurant card
    And I click the quick save button
    Then the page should visually show the SaveToCollectionDialog centered on screen
    And the dialog should display a list of collection checkboxes
    And the homepage content should be visible but dimmed with an overlay opacity of approximately 50%
    And the dialog close button with X icon should be visible in the top-right corner of the dialog
