```gherkin
Feature: Restaurant Quick Actions Menu
  As a user browsing restaurant cards
  I want quick access to common actions via a menu
  So that I can efficiently manage restaurants without navigating away

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"
    And I am on the search page with restaurant cards visible

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Menu button appears on all restaurant cards
    Given I am on the search page
    And at least one restaurant card is visible
    When I examine the first restaurant card
    Then a three-dot menu button should be visible in the top-right corner
    And the button should have data-testid "quick-actions-menu-trigger"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Menu opens when clicking three-dot button
    Given I am on the search page with restaurant cards
    When I click the three-dot menu button on the first restaurant card
    Then a dropdown menu should appear below the button
    And the menu should contain exactly 3 action options
    And the menu should have data-testid "quick-actions-menu"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Copy Link action copies restaurant URL to clipboard
    Given I am on the search page
    And I have opened the quick actions menu on restaurant "rest_0001"
    When I click the "Copy Link" option
    Then the clipboard should contain "http://localhost:3000/restaurant/rest_0001"
    And a success toast should appear with text "Link copied to clipboard"

  @unit-test @priority-high @authenticated
  Scenario: Save to Collection requires authentication and opens dialog when logged in
    Given I am logged in as user "test@example.com"
    And I am on the search page
    And I have opened the quick actions menu on restaurant "rest_0001"
    When I click the "Save to Collection" option
    Then the quick actions menu should close
    And the collection dialog should open with title "Save to Collection"
    And the dialog should show the restaurant name for "rest_0001"

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Menu closes when clicking outside dropdown
    Given I am on the search page
    And I have opened the quick actions menu on the first restaurant card
    And the menu dropdown is visible with 3 options
    When I click on an area outside the menu dropdown
    Then the menu should close immediately
    And the menu should no longer be visible in the DOM

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Multiple menus can open independently without affecting each other
    Given I am on the search page
    And at least 2 restaurant cards are visible
    And I have opened the quick actions menu on the first restaurant card
    When I click the three-dot menu button on the second restaurant card
    Then the menu on the first restaurant card should close
    And the menu on the second restaurant card should be visible with 3 options

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of opened quick actions menu
    Given I am on the search page
    When I click the three-dot menu button on the first visible restaurant card
    Then the page should visually show the dropdown menu open
    And the menu should display three options: "Save to Collection", "Copy Link", "Report Issue"
    And the menu should be positioned below the three-dot button
    And the menu should not overflow the viewport

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of success toast after copying link
    Given I am on the search page
    And I have opened the quick actions menu on a restaurant card
    When I click the "Copy Link" option
    Then the page should visually show a success toast notification
    And the toast should contain the text "Link copied to clipboard"
    And the menu should be closed
```
