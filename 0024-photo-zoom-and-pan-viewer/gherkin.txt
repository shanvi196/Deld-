```gherkin
Feature: Photo Zoom and Pan Viewer
  As a user viewing restaurant photos
  I want to zoom in and pan around photos
  So that I can inspect food and menu details more closely

  Background:
    Given the app is running at "http://localhost:3000"
    And I am on a restaurant details page "/restaurants/rest_0001"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Zoom controls are visible when photo dialog opens
    Given the page has loaded with restaurant photos
    When I click on a menu carousel slide to open the photo dialog
    Then I should see the photo dialog with data-testid "menu-photo-dialog"
    And I should see a zoom in button with data-testid "zoom-in-button"
    And I should see a zoom out button with data-testid "zoom-out-button"
    And I should see a zoom reset button with data-testid "zoom-reset-button"
    And I should see a zoom level indicator with data-testid "zoom-level-indicator" showing "1.0x"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Zoom in button increases zoom level by 0.5x increments up to maximum
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    And the zoom level indicator shows "1.0x"
    When I click the zoom in button once
    Then the zoom level indicator should show "1.5x"
    When I click the zoom in button 3 more times
    Then the zoom level indicator should show "3.0x"
    And the zoom in button should be disabled
    When I click the zoom in button again
    Then the zoom level indicator should still show "3.0x"

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Zoom out and reset buttons are disabled at minimum zoom (1x)
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    And the zoom level is at default "1.0x"
    Then the zoom out button should be disabled
    And the zoom reset button should be disabled
    When I click the zoom in button
    Then the zoom out button should be enabled
    And the zoom reset button should be enabled

  @unit-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Reset button returns zoom to 1x and resets pan position to center
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    And I have zoomed in to "2.0x"
    And I have panned the photo 100px right and 50px down
    When I click the zoom reset button
    Then the zoom level indicator should show "1.0x"
    And the photo transform should be "scale(1) translate(0px, 0px)"
    And the zoom out button should be disabled
    And the zoom reset button should be disabled

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Zoom and pan state resets when navigating to different photo
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    And I have zoomed in to "2.5x"
    And I have panned the photo 150px left and 75px up
    When I click the carousel navigation button to go to the next photo
    Then the zoom level indicator should show "1.0x"
    And the photo transform should be "scale(1) translate(0px, 0px)"
    And the zoomable-photo-container should have cursor style "default"

  @unit-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Zoom and pan state resets when closing and reopening photo dialog
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    And I have zoomed in to "2.0x"
    And I have panned the photo 50px left and 30px up
    When I close the photo dialog by clicking the close button
    And I click on the same menu carousel slide to reopen the photo dialog
    Then the zoom level indicator should show "1.0x"
    And the photo transform should be "scale(1) translate(0px, 0px)"
    And the zoom out button should be disabled
    And the zoom reset button should be disabled

  @screenshot-test @priority-high
  Scenario: Visual validation of zoom controls overlay at default state
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    Then I should see zoom controls positioned in bottom-right corner above the photo counter
    And the zoom in button should display a plus icon and appear enabled
    And the zoom out button should display a minus icon and appear disabled with reduced opacity
    And the zoom reset button should display an expand icon and appear disabled with reduced opacity
    And the zoom level indicator should show "1.0x" in white text

  @screenshot-test @priority-medium
  Scenario: Visual validation of zoomed and panned photo state at maximum zoom
    Given I have opened the photo dialog
    When I click the zoom in button 4 times to reach 3.0x zoom
    Then the photo should appear cropped with CSS transform scale(3)
    And the zoom level indicator should display "3.0x"
    And the zoom in button should appear disabled with reduced opacity
    And the zoom out and reset buttons should appear enabled
    And the zoomable-photo-container should show a grab cursor
```
